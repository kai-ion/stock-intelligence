The dominant story today is Anthropic's $11.6 billion, seven-year cloud infrastructure deal with Akamai — the largest in Akamai's history — which is reshaping how the market prices "surprise AI beneficiaries" and rippling across adjacent names in CDN, compute, and edge infrastructure.
