## Market Overview

Earnings season is delivering a broadly constructive tape, with S&P 500 and Dow futures pointing higher on easing inflation expectations (US 10-year yields hovering near 4.6%) and renewed geopolitical de-escalation hopes around the Strait of Hormuz.
