Forecast: 3.75% | Previous: 3.75%
**Market impact:** The Fed held rates steady at 3.75%, as universally expected, but the tone of the statement and Powell's press conference will be the dominant macro variable entering earnings week. If the statement was more dovish than expected (acknowledging slowing inflation via the 0.1% Core PCE forecast), the equity market should open Monday with a risk-on tilt that benefits high-multiple growth names like PLTR, AMD, ANET, and APP. **Advance GDP q/q** — Thursday, July 30 at 8:30 AM ET *(prior week)*
Forecast: 2.3% | Previous: 2.0%
**Market impact:** A 2.3% GDP print would represent healthy, non-recessionary growth — the Goldilocks scenario for equities. If the actual print came in above 2.5%, watch for bond yields to move higher, which would compress multiples on long-duration growth stocks (PLTR, APP, NET) — a headwind even if macro is "too good." A miss below 2.0% would raise recession fears and punish industrials while benefiting defensive names.

---

**Core PCE Price Index m/m** — Thursday, July 30 at 8:30 AM ET *(prior week)*
Forecast: 0.1% | Previous: 0.3%
**Market impact:** A deceleration from 0.3% to 0.1% in Core PCE would be the single most bullish data point of the month — it validates the Fed's "inflation is cooling" narrative and makes September rate cuts more probable, directly boosting high-growth, high-multiple names. This is the data point that sets the risk appetite ceiling for the entire earnings week. A 0.1% print or below = bull case for AMD, ANET, SHOP, and PLTR. A print above 0.2% would inject uncertainty and potentially cause traders to trim positions ahead of earnings.

---

**Unemployment Claims** — Thursday, July 30 at 8:30 AM ET *(prior week)*
Forecast: 206K | Previous: 187K
**Market impact:** A notable jump from 187K to 206K in initial claims would signal the first meaningful crack in the labor market — watch this carefully. If claims came in above 210K, it would support a Fed cut narrative (good for stocks long-term) but also raise near-term recession fears (bad for consumer-facing names like MCD, BKNG, MELI reporting this week). A print below 195K maintains the "soft landing" narrative and keeps risk-on conditions intact.

**SPCX** — Space Exploration Technologies Corp. | ~$75B offering (IPO filed May 2026)
**Claude's take:** This is the most significant IPO event of 2026, full stop. SpaceX going public at a ~$1.5T implied market cap creates a new reference point for the entire private-to-public market — it validates that mega-cap private companies can debut at scale. Day-1 buyers are almost certainly late to the party given the extraordinary hype cycle; retail investors should absolutely wait for the post-earnings lock-up expiry fade (likely 6 months post-IPO) before initiating long-term positions. The first earnings report (this week) is the first real stress test for new shareholders — if results disappoint expectations priced into that $1.5T cap, the drop could be severe. ---

**CBRS** — Cerebras Systems Inc. | $5.55B offering | Priced $185.00 on 5/14/2026
**Claude's take:** Cerebras makes purpose-built AI chips (wafer-scale processors) that are genuinely differentiated from NVIDIA's GPU architecture — its CS-3 chip is optimized for large language model inference, a fast-growing workload that NVIDIA's H100/H200 weren't originally designed for. This is a legitimate pure-play AI hardware bet, but at $5.55B offering size it's extremely richly valued for a company still in early commercial revenue ramp. Day-1 buyers likely got a pop but the stock is now subject to post-IPO registration statement overhang. I'd wait for the 90-day lockup expiry to find a more attractive entry, or watch for any broader semiconductor selloff that brings this down to a better risk/reward level.

---

**BXDC** — Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust Inc. | $1.75B offering | Priced $20.00 on 5/14/2026
**Claude's take:** Blackstone bringing a dedicated digital infrastructure REIT to market is a savvy institutional move — it packages data center and fiber infrastructure assets into a yield vehicle right as AI power/compute demand is peaking. The $20 NAV pricing suggests moderate initial valuation, and the Blackstone brand provides institutional credibility and deal flow access. This is more of a buy-and-hold income vehicle than a swing trade — the dividend yield and NAV growth will be the long-term return drivers. If the stock has pulled back from IPO price, it's interesting as a yield play; don't chase if it's trading above $22.

---

**FRVO** — Fervo Energy Co | $1.89B offering | Priced $27.00 on 5/13/2026
**Claude's take:** Fervo is the most interesting energy IPO in years — it's a next-generation geothermal energy developer using horizontal drilling technology (borrowed from the oil patch) to dramatically expand geothermal's addressable geography beyond traditional volcanic regions. With AI data centers consuming enormous quantities of always-on baseload power, Fervo's clean, dispatchable geothermal electricity has signed significant offtake agreements with hyperscalers. This is a legitimate long-term holding, but it's pre-revenue scale — the stock will be highly volatile on early operational results. Wait for a post-lockup pullback to the $20–$22 range before building a position; the geothermal story is compelling over a 3–5 year horizon.

---

**LCLN** — Lincoln International Inc. | $421M offering | Priced $20.00 on 5/20/2026
**Claude's take:** Lincoln International is a middle-market investment bank (M&A advisory, restructuring) — a business that's extremely cyclical and sensitive to deal flow volumes. If the M&A cycle is recovering (as suggested by improving credit markets and strategic deal activity in 2026), Lincoln should benefit, but the stock is competing for attention with more exciting growth narratives. Day-1 buyers of financial advisory IPOs historically see modest first-year returns; this is a 12–18 month hold story contingent on M&A volume recovery. Pass for now and revisit if the deal environment meaningfully improves in H2 2026.

Earnings breadth should be net positive given the AI infrastructure cycle, travel demand resilience, and NAND/HDD upcycle confirmation. Run a barbell: core longs in AMD/ANET/CEG/BKNG paired with tight stops, while avoiding "priced for perfection" names like PLTR, APP, and CAT until after their prints clear.
