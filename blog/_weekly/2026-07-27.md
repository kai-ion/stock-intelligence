- 🔴 **Biggest Risk:** The FOMC on Wednesday afternoon lands right in the middle of the most important earnings week of the year — MSFT and META report the same evening as the rate decision. If Powell strikes a more hawkish tone than expected (pointing to the hot GDP Price Index forecast of 4.1%), you could see a violent market dislocation that overshadows even strong earnings beats from the mega-caps. Have defined-risk positions, not naked exposure, heading into Wednesday 2 PM ET.

- 🟢 **Biggest Opportunity:** The MSFT → META → AAPL → AMZN earnings gauntlet represents the largest single-week concentration of AI earnings firepower ever assembled. If even two of these four names beat and raise — which is historically more likely than not — the resulting market momentum could push QQQ to new all-time highs by Friday. The highest-conviction trade of the week is being long quality AI infrastructure names (MSFT, VRT, LRCX, GLW) before their respective prints.

- 📊 **Key Levels to Watch:**
  - **SPY:** Watch the $590–595 support zone — a dovish FOMC + MSFT/META beat setup keeps SPY above $600; a hawkish surprise could retest $585.
  - **QQQ:** The $520 level is critical support; a clean break above $530 on the back of mega-cap earnings beats opens the door to $545–550 by end of week.
  - **VIX:** Currently likely elevated into this week — any drop below 15 post-FOMC would be a green light to add risk; a spike above 22 would warrant immediate defensive rotation.

- 🏦 **Thursday Morning Data Dump is the Hidden Landmine:** GDP + Core PCE + GDP Price Index + Unemployment Claims all hitting at 8:30 AM Thursday — hours before Apple and Amazon close — means pre-market Thursday is the most data-dense moment of the entire week. The GDP Price Index at 4.1% is the number I'm most worried about; if it comes in hotter, the bond market will sell off hard and that pressure will bleed directly into growth stocks at exactly the wrong moment.

- ⚖️ **Overall Positioning: Cautiously Risk-On, with Defined Risk.** This is a week to be long the highest-quality names with clear earnings catalysts (MSFT, AAPL, AMZN, VRT, KLAC) while keeping position sizes smaller than usual into Wednesday's FOMC. Use options where available to define downside, avoid leveraged positions through the Tuesday-Wednesday gauntlet, and be ready to add aggressively on Thursday morning if GDP and Core PCE data come in on the friendly side of estimates. The path of least resistance is higher — but the volatility risk is real, and this is a week where hedging isn't paranoia, it's professional discipline.
